# Ruinbound Gear Archetypes

## Design Goal

Ruinbound has three major gear archetypes. These archetypes do not lock equipment to a particular character class. Instead, the equipment a player chooses determines whether their build leans toward ability damage, basic attacks, or durability. Players may also mix pieces from different archetypes.

## Archetype Overview

| Gear archetype | Defense | Primary offense | Intended playstyle |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Arcanist Gear** | Low | Ability damage, cooldown recovery, and resource generation | Frequent and powerful ability use |
| **Striker Gear** | Medium | Basic-attack damage, attack speed, and critical hits | Weapon-focused sustained damage |
| **Bulwark Gear** | High | Damage that scales from defensive stats and defensive actions | Durable frontline combat and retaliation |

The proposed armor slots are **head, chest, gloves, legs, and boots**. Weapons and accessories can later use the same three archetypes.

---

## Arcanist Gear

Arcanist Gear is light armor intended for spellcasters and ability-heavy builds. It provides the highest ability damage of the three archetypes, but its low defenses make mistakes more dangerous.

### Common: Apprentice Set

Common Arcanist pieces provide straightforward ability improvements with very little protection.

- **Apprentice Hood:** Increased ability damage.
- **Apprentice Robes:** A small amount of defense and increased maximum ability resource.
- **Apprentice Gloves:** Increased ability-resource generation.
- **Apprentice Leggings:** Increased ability damage.
- **Apprentice Shoes:** Increased cooldown recovery.

### Rare: Runebound Set

Rare Arcanist pieces introduce bonuses that trigger through active ability use.

- **Runebound Hood:** Ability critical hits deal increased damage.
- **Runebound Robes:** Using an ability grants a small temporary barrier.
- **Runebound Gloves:** Damaging an enemy with an ability restores ability resource.
- **Runebound Leggings:** Consecutive ability hits against the same enemy gain damage.
- **Runebound Shoes:** Using an ability briefly increases movement speed.

### Epic: Spellweaver Set

Epic Arcanist pieces cause abilities to interact with one another instead of providing only numerical bonuses.

- **Spellweaver Crown — Arcane Rhythm:** Every third ability deals bonus damage.
- **Spellweaver Mantle — Warding Invocation:** Using a defensive ability strengthens the next offensive ability.
- **Spellweaver Grips — Overflowing Power:** Ability resource gained above its maximum becomes a temporary barrier.
- **Spellweaver Legwraps — Lingering Sorcery:** Ability damage leaves a short damage-over-time effect.
- **Spellweaver Steps — Quickened Casting:** Defeating an enemy with an ability reduces active cooldowns.

### Legendary Arcanist Gear

- **Crown of the Final Word — Echoed Invocation:** After using three different abilities, the next ability is cast a second time at reduced power.
- **Robes of the Hollow Star — Desperate Conduit:** Ability damage increases as health decreases. Using an ability while critically wounded grants a barrier, subject to a cooldown.
- **Hands of the Spellthief — Stolen Power:** Hitting an elite or boss with an ability temporarily copies a portion of its offensive strength. Repeated hits refresh the effect but do not stack it.
- **Boots of the Riftwalker — Rift Step:** Dodging leaves behind a rift that erupts after a short delay and deals ability damage.

## Striker Gear

Striker Gear is medium armor for weapon-focused and basic-attack builds. It provides more defense than Arcanist Gear without approaching Bulwark survivability.

### Common: Raider Set

Common Striker pieces provide dependable weapon bonuses with reasonable protection.

- **Raider Helm:** Increased basic-attack damage.
- **Raider Vest:** Moderate defense.
- **Raider Gloves:** Increased attack speed.
- **Raider Legguards:** Increased critical-hit chance.
- **Raider Boots:** Increased movement speed while attacking.

### Rare: Duelist Set

Rare Striker pieces reward positioning, mobility, and continued attacking.

- **Duelist Helm:** Basic attacks against undamaged enemies deal bonus damage.
- **Duelist Vest:** Attacking shortly after dodging grants temporary defense.
- **Duelist Gloves:** Consecutive basic attacks gradually increase attack speed.
- **Duelist Legguards:** Critical basic attacks briefly increase movement speed.
- **Duelist Boots:** Basic attacks made shortly after sprinting deal bonus damage.

### Epic: Executioner Set

Epic Striker pieces create powerful attack sequences that enemies can interrupt.

- **Executioner Visor — Expose Weakness:** Every fifth basic attack makes the target temporarily take increased basic-attack damage.
- **Executioner Harness — Battle Momentum:** Sustained basic attacks grant stacking damage resistance. The stacks quickly disappear when attacking stops.
- **Executioner Grips — Relentless Hands:** Critical basic attacks accelerate the next several attacks.
- **Executioner Legguards — Predator's Patience:** Avoiding damage for a short period increases the damage of the next basic attack.
- **Executioner Treads — Pursuit:** Moving toward an injured enemy increases movement speed and basic-attack damage.

### Legendary Striker Gear

- **Helm of the Perfect Hunt — Marked for Death:** The first basic attack marks an enemy. Continued basic attacks build toward a powerful finishing strike.
- **Bloodrush Harness — Unbroken Assault:** Basic attacks build Momentum, increasing attack speed and damage. Taking a heavy hit removes some Momentum instead of all of it.
- **Gauntlets of the Twin Fang — Phantom Strike:** Every few basic attacks produces a spectral follow-up attack. Slow weapons trigger it less often but create a stronger follow-up.
- **Wayfarer's Killing Boots — Drive-By:** Dodging through or past an enemy empowers the next basic attack with bonus damage and increased reach.

## Bulwark Gear

Bulwark Gear is heavy armor with substantially greater defense. Its offense scales from Armor, maximum health, blocking, barriers, and other defensive actions.

Bulwark Gear should not receive large amounts of passive damage merely for being equipped. Its strongest offensive bonuses should require the player to block, absorb, resist, or survive attacks.

### Common: Ironwall Set

Common Bulwark pieces provide straightforward survivability and minor defense-based offense.

- **Ironwall Helm:** Increased Armor.
- **Ironwall Plate:** Increased maximum health.
- **Ironwall Gauntlets:** Basic attacks gain a small damage bonus based on Armor.
- **Ironwall Legplates:** Increased stagger resistance.
- **Ironwall Greaves:** Reduced knockback and improved stability.

### Rare: Bastion Set

Rare Bulwark pieces turn enemy pressure into defensive and offensive opportunities.

- **Bastion Helm:** Taking a heavy hit briefly increases Armor.
- **Bastion Plate:** Healing received is increased while below half health.
- **Bastion Gauntlets:** Blocking empowers the next basic attack.
- **Bastion Legplates:** Remaining near multiple enemies grants damage resistance.
- **Bastion Greaves:** Resisting a stagger releases a small damaging shockwave.

### Epic: Juggernaut Set

Epic Bulwark pieces actively convert defense into pressure, stagger, and retaliation.

- **Juggernaut Greathelm — Immovable:** Remaining in place for a short time builds Armor and stagger resistance. Moving gradually removes the effect.
- **Juggernaut Plate — Retaliating Steel:** A portion of damage mitigated by Armor charges a damaging pulse.
- **Juggernaut Fists — Crushing Weight:** Heavy attacks gain damage based on Armor.
- **Juggernaut Legplates — Hold the Line:** Nearby enemies increase defense and the size of retaliatory effects.
- **Juggernaut Sabatons — Unstoppable Advance:** Moving toward enemies reduces slows and builds stagger power for the next attack.

### Legendary Bulwark Gear

- **Crown of the Unbroken — Refuse to Fall:** Fatal damage instead leaves the player at one health and creates a barrier based on maximum health. This effect has a long cooldown.
- **Worldbearer Plate — Living Fortress:** A portion of bonus Armor also increases attack damage. The conversion has a cap to prevent extreme Armor stacking from producing excessive damage.
- **Gauntlets of Reprisal — Answer the Blow:** Blocking or heavily mitigating an attack stores part of the prevented damage. The next heavy attack releases the stored power.
- **Graveshaker Sabatons — Weight of War:** After traveling a certain distance, the next heavy attack creates a shockwave. Maximum health increases its area, while Armor increases its stagger.

## Rarity Structure

Rarity should change more than the size of an item's numbers.

| Rarity | Design purpose | Recommended affixes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Common** | Establishes the item's basic role | One straightforward stat bonus |
| **Rare** | Adds a conditional combat bonus | Two stats, or one stat and one small effect |
| **Epic** | Supports or defines a particular build loop | Two or three stats and one epic effect |
| **Legendary** | Changes how the player approaches combat | Two or three stats and one unique legendary power |

## Balance Rules

- Arcanist Gear should provide approximately **60–70% of Striker Gear's defense**.
- Striker Gear should provide approximately **70–80% of Bulwark Gear's defense**.
- Bulwark's passive damage should remain below Striker damage.
- Bulwark builds may temporarily approach Striker damage after successfully blocking or mitigating attacks.
- Arcanist should offer the strongest burst damage.
- Striker should offer the strongest sustained basic-attack damage.
- Bulwark should offer the safest sustained combat.
- Damage conversions based on defensive stats need caps.
- Defensive damage conversions should generally use **bonus Armor** or **bonus Health**, preventing base-stat scaling from becoming excessive.
- Legendary effects should normally have cooldowns, buildup requirements, or conditional triggers.
- Slow and fast weapons must receive equivalent value from effects triggered by a number of basic attacks. Slow weapons may trigger these effects less frequently but should produce stronger results.

## Core Identities

- **Arcanist:** Turns abilities into combos.
- **Striker:** Turns basic attacks into momentum.
- **Bulwark:** Turns enemy pressure into retaliation.

## Future Design Work

- Choose exact stat ranges for each rarity and gear slot.
- Decide how Armor, barriers, stagger resistance, and ability resource are calculated.
- Design weapons and accessories for all three archetypes.
- Decide whether wearing multiple pieces from one archetype grants set bonuses.
- Establish loot weights and item-level scaling.
- Playtest defensive-stat damage conversions and define their caps.
